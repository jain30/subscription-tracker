Privacy Policy

Application: Subscription Tracker  |  Effective Date: November 2, 2026  |  Last Updated: November 2, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Subscription Tracker ("we", "our", or "the app"). Subscription Tracker is a personal subscription and recurring expense tracking application that helps users organize their recurring subscriptions in one place, calculate upcoming renewal costs, and manage billing dates.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, where it is stored, how it is protected, and how you can exercise complete control over your data, including deleting your account and records.

We operate from India and adhere strictly to Google Play Developer Program Policies, including the Google Play User Data and Data Safety guidelines.

2. Information We Collect

Subscription Tracker collects only the minimal data necessary to provide its core budgeting and account services:

A. Account & Identity Information

When you create an account within the app, we collect:

  • Full Name: Stored to personalize your account profile header and settings within the application.
  • Email Address: Used as your unique account identifier for authentication, account security, and password recovery.
  • Password: Transmitted securely via TLS-encrypted connections directly to Supabase Authentication. Passwords are cryptographically hashed using industry-standard bcrypt algorithms. We never store or have access to your plaintext password.

B. Subscription & Expense Information

To help you track your recurring obligations, you may enter:

  • Service Name: Name of the subscription or provider (e.g., Netflix, Spotify).
  • Price & Currency: The renewal amount and currency token (e.g., INR, USD, EUR, GBP, CAD).
  • Billing Frequency: Weekly, monthly, or yearly renewal cycles.
  • Renewal Dates: The initial billing date and upcoming renewal date.
  • Category & Status: Classification category (e.g., Entertainment, Productivity) and subscription status (Active, Trial, Canceled).
  • Settings & Preferences: Auto-renew preferences and notification reminder offsets.
  • Personal Notes: Optional custom notes you choose to attach to a subscription.

3. Financial Information Disclaimer

Important Financial Notice: Subscription Tracker is strictly a personal organization and expense tracking utility. We do not collect credit card numbers, debit card numbers, CVVs, expiration dates, or bank account credentials. We do not connect to bank accounts, do not process financial transactions, and do not offer lending, money transfer, or financial services.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to provide the core functionality of Subscription Tracker:

  • Creating, managing, and authenticating your user account.
  • Displaying your personalized dashboard, upcoming payment timelines, and category breakdowns.
  • Calculating monthly and yearly recurring subscription expense projections.
  • Synchronizing your subscription entries securely across your devices.
  • Sending account-related transactional emails (such as verification links and password recovery codes).
  • Executing account and data deletion upon your request.

Zero Commercial Sharing: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or share your personal information or financial tracking records with any advertisers, ad networks, or data brokers.

6. Data Security & Storage Architecture

We implement multi-layered technical measures to ensure your information is safeguarded:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between the mobile application and our servers is secured using modern TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption.
  • Encrypted Device Storage: On Android devices, authentication session tokens are encrypted with AES-256 prior to local storage. Encryption keys are securely stored in the Android Keystore via Expo SecureStore.
  • PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS): The database strictly enforces Row Level Security. Only your authenticated user account (`auth.uid() = user_id`) can access or modify your records. Cross-account access is cryptographically blocked.
  • In-Memory Flush: Logging out or deleting your account immediately purges all subscription records from the device's volatile memory.

7. Data Retention Policy

We retain your account and subscription information only while your account remains active and in good standing.

  • Active Accounts: Maintained on secure servers to provide real-time synchronization.
  • Deleted Accounts: When an account is deleted, all profile rows and subscription records are instantly and permanently purged from active production databases via database cascade triggers.
  • Backups: Standard operational disaster recovery snapshots are retained for standard operational periods (up to 30 days) and are permanently purged in accordance with automated lifecycle policies.

8. Account and Data Deletion (User Rights)

You maintain full control over your data and can permanently delete your account and all associated subscription records at any time:

Option 1: In-App Deletion (Instant & Real-Time)

  1. Open Subscription Tracker on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Settings tab from the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Account Actions section.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm your deletion request in the confirmation sheet.

Result: Your account, authentication credentials, profile row, and all recorded subscriptions are permanently erased from our production servers in real time.

Option 2: Web Deletion Request (If App is Uninstalled)

If you no longer have access to the mobile application, you can submit an account and data deletion request via our public web page or directly by email:

  • Web Page: Account Deletion Request Page
  • Email Request: Send an email to jainpooja.vit@gmail.com from the email address registered with your account.
  • Subject: Account Deletion Request - Subscription Tracker
  • Processing Time: Verified email requests are processed and confirmed within 30 days.
