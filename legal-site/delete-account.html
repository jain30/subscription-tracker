Google Play Data Safety Resource

Delete Account & Personal Data

Application: Subscription Tracker  |  Developer: Pooja Jain

We respect your privacy and provide you with complete control over your personal data. In compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies, you can permanently delete your account and all associated personal information at any time.

Warning: Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. When your account is deleted, your profile, authentication credentials, and all recorded subscription entries are permanently purged from our servers.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the Subscription Tracker application installed on your device, you can delete your account immediately in real time:

  1. Open the Subscription Tracker app on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Settings tab located in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Account Actions section.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Read the confirmation warning and tap Delete Account to confirm.

Your authentication credentials, user profile row, and all recorded subscription data are immediately deleted from our production database via database cascade triggers.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request (If App is Uninstalled)

If you no longer have access to the mobile application or your device, you can submit an account and data deletion request directly to the developer by email:

Email your request to: jainpooja.vit@gmail.com

Subject line: Account Deletion Request - Subscription Tracker

Verification requirement: Please send the email from the exact email address registered with your Subscription Tracker account so we can verify account ownership.

What Data is Deleted?

Upon account deletion (whether executed inside the app or requested via email), the following data is permanently purged:

  • Account Identity & Profile: Your user identity record in Supabase Auth, email address, display name, and all authentication tokens.
  • Subscription Records: All subscription names, prices, currencies, billing frequencies, renewal dates, categories, and custom notes.
  • Device State: All locally encrypted authentication tokens in your device's Android Keystore and AsyncStorage are cleared.

Data Retention & Processing Timeline

  • In-app deletion: Processed instantly in real time.
  • Email deletion requests: Verified and permanently executed within 30 days of receipt. No user data is retained after this processing window.
  • Backups: Routine disaster recovery snapshots are automatically cycled and purged within 30 days.
