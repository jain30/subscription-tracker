1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Subscription Tracker ("we", "our", or "the app"). Subscription Tracker is a personal subscription and recurring expense management application designed to help users track renewals, organize recurring budgets, and view payment schedules.

This Privacy Policy explains in transparent, plain language what data we collect, why we collect it, how it is stored and protected, and how you can exercise full control over your personal data, including complete account and data deletion.

We operate from India and adhere to Google Play Developer Program Policies, including the Google Play User Data and Data Safety guidelines.

2. Information We Collect

Subscription Tracker collects only the minimal information necessary to deliver core account and budgeting functionality:

A. Account & Profile Information

When you create an account within the app, we collect:

  • Full Name: Used solely as your display name in your personal settings and profile header.
  • Email Address: Used as your unique account identifier for authentication, account security, and password recovery.
  • Password & Credentials: Submitted securely via encrypted channels directly to our authentication provider (Supabase Auth). We never store or view your plaintext password.

B. Subscription & Expense Information

To help you track your recurring expenses, you may enter:

  • Subscription Name & Service: (e.g., Netflix, Spotify, Cloud Storage).
  • Cost & Currency: The renewal price and currency code (e.g., INR, USD, EUR, GBP, CAD).
  • Billing Frequency: Weekly, monthly, or yearly billing cycles.
  • Renewal Dates: The first charge date and next expected renewal date.
  • Category & Status: Service category (e.g., Entertainment, Productivity) and status (Active, Trial, Canceled).
  • Preferences: Auto-renew toggles and reminder offset preferences.
  • Personal Notes: Optional user-provided reference notes.

3. Financial & Payment Information Disclaimer

Crucial Security Clarification: Subscription Tracker is strictly a manual expense-tracking and personal organization tool. We do not collect credit card numbers, debit card numbers, CVVs, expiration dates, or bank account credentials. We do not connect to bank APIs, do not process financial transactions, and do not offer lending, money transfer, or banking services.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively for providing and maintaining app functionality:

  • Authenticating your account and securing access across your devices.
  • Displaying your personalized dashboard, renewal timelines, and expense totals.
  • Calculating upcoming payments, monthly projections, and spending insights.
  • Delivering account-related transactional emails (such as password reset codes).
  • Executing account modifications or account deletion upon your request.

No Commercial Exploitation: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal or financial tracking data to any third parties, data brokers, or advertisers.

6. Data Security & Storage Architecture

We implement strict multi-layered technical controls to safeguard your data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the mobile application and Supabase cloud servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Encrypted Device Storage: On Android devices, authentication session tokens are encrypted with AES-256 prior to storage. Cryptographic keys are maintained securely in the Android Keystore via Expo SecureStore.
  • Row-Level Security (RLS): The database enforces strict PostgreSQL Row Level Security. Only you (verified via cryptographically signed JWT tokens) can read, modify, or delete your subscriptions. Cross-user access is impossible at the database engine level.
  • Zero In-Memory Retention on Signout: Signing out or deleting your account immediately flushes all subscription records and credentials from device memory.

7. Data Retention Policy

We retain your account and subscription records only for as long as your account remains active.

  • Active Accounts: Stored securely to provide seamless multi-device synchronization.
  • Deleted Accounts: When an account is deleted, all related personal records (profile and subscription entries) are immediately and permanently removed from our active production database via database-level cascade triggers.
  • Backups: Routine disaster recovery backups are retained for standard operational periods (up to 30 days) and are purged through automated lifecycle policies.

8. Account and Data Deletion (User Rights)

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies and global privacy laws, you have full authority to permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Immediate)

  1. Open Subscription Tracker on your mobile device.
  2. Navigate to the Settings tab from the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Account Actions section.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm your choice in the verification dialog.

Outcome: Your account, authentication credentials, profile row, and all recorded subscriptions are permanently and irreversibly purged from our servers in real time.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request (If App is Uninstalled)

If you no longer have access to the mobile application, you can submit an account and data deletion request via our public deletion page or by email:
