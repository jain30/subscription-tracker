Delete Your Subscription Tracker Account

We respect your privacy and give you full control over your personal data. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time either directly from within the app or by submitting an email request.

Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your tracked subscriptions, payment reminders, settings, and profile information will be irreversibly erased.

Option 1: Instant Deletion Inside the App (Recommended)

You can instantly and permanently delete your account directly inside the Subscription Tracker mobile application:

  1. Open the Subscription Tracker app on your device.
  2. Go to the Settings tab from the bottom navigation.
  3. Scroll down to the Account Actions section.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Review the confirmation dialog and confirm by tapping Delete Account.

Your authentication credentials, user profile, and all subscription records will be immediately and permanently deleted from our servers.

Option 2: Request Deletion via Email (Web Request)

If you no longer have access to the app or device, you can request manual deletion by contacting our support team:

Send an email to: jainpooja.vit@gmail.com

Subject line: Account Deletion Request - Subscription Tracker

Requirements: Please send the request from the email address registered with your Subscription Tracker account so we can verify ownership.

What Data is Deleted?

Upon account deletion (whether performed inside the app or requested via email), the following data is permanently purged from our databases:

  • Account Credentials & Profile: Your user identity, email address, display name, and authentication tokens.
  • Subscription Records: All subscription names, pricing, currencies, billing frequencies, renewal dates, and notes.
  • Reminders & Preferences: All notification configurations and application preferences.

Retention and Processing Timeframe

  • In-app deletion: Processed instantly in real time.
  • Email deletion requests: Verified and permanently processed within 30 days of receiving the email request. No residual user data or backups are retained beyond this timeframe.
